# Chapter 5 — Rider App

The Rider App is for couriers who deliver orders and take home measurements on behalf of tailor shops.

## Getting started

- Register with phone number + OTP
- Complete profile: name, vehicle details, contact information
- Upload required documents (ID, driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance)
- Submit for **Mgask approval** — riders cannot take jobs until verified

## Working with tailor shops

Riders do not pick jobs from an open marketplace by default. Instead:

1. A tailor generates an invitation code
2. The rider enters the code in the Rider App
3. The rider joins that tailor's team
4. The tailor controls whether the rider can do **deliveries**, **home measurements**, or both

A rider can belong to more than one tailor team.

## Daily work

| Task | What the rider does |
|------|---------------------|
| View available jobs | See orders ready for pickup or delivery |
| Accept a job | Confirm assignment |
| Home measurement | Travel to customer, take body measurements, confirm styles with customer if needed |
| Pickup from tailor | Collect finished order from the shop |
| Deliver to customer | Hand over the order and mark as delivered |
| Live location | GPS updates so the customer can track progress on a map |

## Measurements and customer consent

When taking measurements at a customer's home:

- Use the standard measurement form (same fields the tailor uses in shop)
- If a style change is needed, request **customer approval** through the app before proceeding
- The customer receives a notification to confirm or decline

## Earnings

- Riders earn a **delivery fee** when a delivery is completed
- Riders earn a **measurement fee** when a home measurement visit is completed
- Earnings appear in the rider's in-app wallet
- Riders can request a **payout** (bank transfer) which Mgask processes after approval
