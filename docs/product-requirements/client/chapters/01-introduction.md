# Chapter 1 — Introduction

## What is Mgask?

**Mgask** is a digital tailoring platform built for Saudi Arabia. It connects customers who want custom thobes and fabrics with tailor shops, and helps those shops manage orders, staff, and deliveries — all through mobile apps.

Whether a customer orders from home or visits a shop in person, Mgask brings the full tailoring experience into one connected system.

## What problems does Mgask solve?

| For customers | For tailor shops |
|---------------|------------------|
| Find trusted tailors and browse fabrics online | Manage orders digitally instead of paper and phone calls |
| Order stitching with custom styles and measurements | Track every order from acceptance to delivery |
| Pay online and follow delivery on a map | Run multiple branches and staff from one owner account |
| Save family members' measurements for repeat orders | Accept walk-in customers at the shop counter (POS) |

## The three mobile apps

Mgask is delivered through three apps, each designed for a specific type of user:

| App | Who uses it |
|-----|-------------|
| **Customer App** | People who buy fabrics and order thobe stitching |
| **Tailor App** | Shop owners, shop staff, and solo tailors |
| **Rider App** | Delivery and home-measurement couriers |

A single platform administrator team (Mgask operations) oversees shop approvals, rider verification, and platform settings behind the scenes.

## Document purpose

This document describes **what Mgask does** and **how people use it**. It is written for business stakeholders and partners who need a clear picture of the live platform without technical detail.
