# Chapter 7 — Payments and Delivery

## Payment methods

| Method | When used | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| **Online card payment** | Customer checkout | Secure payment through supported Saudi payment gateways |
| **Cash on delivery** | Home delivery orders | Customer pays the rider or upon delivery; tailor may decline if needed |
| **Cash in shop** | Walk-in (POS) orders | Payment at the counter; cash stays at the shop |
| **Partial payment** | Any online order | Pay a deposit now; pay the remaining balance later through the app |

## What the customer sees at checkout

Before confirming, the customer sees a clear breakdown:

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Fabric price | Cost of selected fabrics |
| Stitching price | Tailoring labour |
| Delivery fee | Based on distance; may be free above a minimum order value |
| Express fee | Optional faster service charge |
| Measurement fee | One-time charge if a home measurement visit is required |
| Tax (VAT) | Applied as per Saudi regulations |
| **Total** | Final amount to pay |

## Free home measurement

Each customer account includes **one free home measurement** for eligible orders. After that, the shop's measurement fee applies.

## Delivery and tracking

For home delivery orders:

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Live map tracking | Customer sees where the rider is in real time |
| Estimated arrival | Approximate time and distance shown |
| Status updates | Notifications at each stage (accepted, on the way, delivered) |
| Delivery history | Route recorded for a limited period for support purposes |

Delivery fees may be waived when the order total exceeds a platform threshold set by Mgask.

## Tailor and rider earnings

| Who | How they get paid |
|-----|-------------------|
| **Tailor (home delivery orders)** | Earnings credited to an in-app wallet after the order is completed and paid |
| **Tailor (walk-in cash)** | Cash received directly at the shop |
| **Rider** | Delivery fee and/or measurement fee credited to rider wallet after job completion |
| **Payout** | Tailors and riders request a bank transfer from their wallet; Mgask processes approved requests |

## Refunds

If a payment issue or cancellation requires a refund, it is handled according to platform policy and the payment method used.
