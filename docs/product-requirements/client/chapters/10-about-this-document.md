# Chapter 10 — About This Document

## Purpose

This overview describes the **Mgask platform as it exists today** — the features, user roles, and workflows that are already built and in use.

It is intended for business partners, investors, and anyone who needs a clear understanding of the product **without technical or development detail**.

## What is covered

- Who uses Mgask and what each app does
- How customers place and receive orders
- How tailor shops and riders operate on the platform
- Payments, delivery, and staff access at a high level
